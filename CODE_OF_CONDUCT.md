# Code of conduct

PairClear repos are about relationships and feelings, so people who open an issue may be going through something hard. Please be kind.

## Expected

- Be respectful and patient, especially with people who write about their own situation.
- Discuss the text or the code, not the person.
- Accept that people disagree about schools of psychology; bring sources, not insults.

## Not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, sexual content, threats, or discrimination of any kind.
- Diagnosing or labelling other participants or their partners.
- Posting anyone's private information or messages without consent.
- Using issues to ask for therapy or crisis help from strangers. If you are in danger, call emergency services (112 / 911) or find a helpline at [findahelpline.com](https://findahelpline.com).

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide or delete comments, close issues and block accounts that break these rules. Report a problem privately to **insk.ma@gmail.com**; reports are kept confidential.

Adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/), version 2.1.
