# Support

- **A bug or a wrong text in a tool:** open an issue in that repo.
- **A question about how to use a tool:** open an issue with the "Question" label, or read the FAQ in the repo's README.
- **Articles and quizzes about relationships:** [pairclear.com](https://pairclear.com/en/?utm_source=github&utm_medium=support&utm_campaign=org).

**This is not a crisis service, and issues are public.** If you are thinking about hurting yourself, or you are afraid of your partner, call emergency services (112 in Europe, 911 in the US and Canada) or find a free helpline at [findahelpline.com](https://findahelpline.com).
