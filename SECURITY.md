# Security and privacy

The PairClear tools are static pages: no server, no accounts, no analytics. Entries stay in your browser's localStorage.

If you find a way a tool could leak what a person typed (a network request, a third-party script, an export that includes more than expected), or a crisis number that is wrong, please **do not open a public issue**. Email **insk.ma@gmail.com** with the repo, the steps and what you expected. We aim to reply within 7 days.

Wrong or outdated helpline numbers can also be reported publicly if you prefer: speed matters more than privacy there.
